# Superstore-Sales-Analysis
# 📊 Sales Data Analysis (SQL and Looker Project)

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on analysing retail sales data using SQL to uncover key business insights. The goal was to understand revenue trends, customer behaviour, and product performance to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyse overall sales performance
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Understand customer purchasing behaviour
* Explore sales trends over time

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **SQL (MySQL Workbench)**
* **Kaggle Dataset (Superstore Sales Data)**

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

Before analysis, the dataset was cleaned to ensure accuracy and consistency:

* Standardised column names (removed spaces and improved readability)
* Checked for and handled missing values
* Identified and removed duplicate records where necessary
* Converted data types (e.g., dates and numeric fields)

---

## 📊 Key Analysis

### 💰 Total Revenue

Calculated total sales to understand overall business performance.

### 🏆 Top Products

Identified the highest revenue-generating products.

### 📦 Sales by Category

Analysed which product categories contribute the most to revenue.

### 📅 Monthly Sales Trends

Examined how sales vary over time to identify seasonal patterns.

### 👤 Top Customers

Identified high-value customers based on total spending.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* A small group of customers contributes significantly to total revenue
* Certain product categories outperform others consistently
* Sales trends show variation across months, indicating possible seasonality
* High-performing products can be prioritised for marketing and inventory planning

---

## 📈 Business Impact

The insights from this analysis can help businesses:

* Optimise product offerings
* Improve customer targeting strategies
* Plan inventory based on demand trends
* Support data-driven decision-making

---
